After a brief pause, CSD Express is back with a sharper operating model.

Core services

What we move, and how.

Five ways to put cargo on our corridors. Every option is booked against a schedule, manifested, and tracked to the dock.

Part loadPTL

Share truck space on a scheduled corridor run. Consolidated route-wise at the Bengaluru hub, manifested and dispatched the same night.

Full truck loadFTL

A dedicated vehicle for your consignment, loaded at your dock and sealed through to destination. No shared space, no intermediate handling.

WarehousingBengaluru hub

Short-term holding, sorting and route-wise staging before dispatch, with every carton scanned in and scanned out.

Trailer servicesFlatbed and low-bed

Machinery, structures and over-dimensional cargo on flatbed and low-bed trailers, with route planning done before the vehicle is allocated.

Container movement20 ft and 40 ft

Container haulage between inland depots, ports and your plant, booked against the same corridor discipline as everything else we run.
